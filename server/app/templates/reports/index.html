Reports

Every generated report is recorded in the export history with its filters, record count and a SHA-256 hash of the file. Sections are labelled RECORDED (stored as it happened), CALCULATED (derived from recorded readings) or INFERRED (estimated from missing data). No report claims compliance with a regulatory standard.

Downtime Node downtime inferred from missing heartbeats, plus recorded communication events.
